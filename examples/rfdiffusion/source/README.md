# Public paper sources

This presentation discusses Watson, Juergens, Bennett et al., *De novo design of protein structure and function with RFdiffusion*, Nature 620, 1089–1100 (2023), DOI [10.1038/s41586-023-06415-8](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06415-8).

[Official article and supplementary-information links](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06415-8). The complete original PDFs are not duplicated in this package. References to `source/main.pdf` and the supplementary PDF in asset/source maps identify the original documents used during preparation; use the DOI page to obtain them. PDF page numbers, panel IDs and normalized crop coordinates remain in `asset-map.json`.

The article's page 12 states Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International, © The Author(s) 2023. A full permission notice transcribed from the article is retained in `article-permissions.txt`. Article images are covered unless a separate credit line indicates otherwise. The selected primary research figure crops are attributed to the article; no private research images are used.
